Why HueKnow

Accurate color analysis

HueKnow extracts dominant colors client-side and calculates exact pixel coverage percentages.

Instant results

Processing happens entirely in your browser with zero network latency.

No account needed

Two ways to view it

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. All color extraction takes place locally inside your web browser.

What file types are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WEBP. Transparent areas in PNGs are ignored when calculating the palette.

How are color names decided?

HueKnow maps RGB values to human-readable names using HSL color space analysis.

Can I use this for a design palette?

Yes. Click any swatch to copy its hex code, then drop it straight into your design tool of choice.
